## Prompt

PwC has recently been appointed as the strategic transformation partner for StyleVerse Global, one of India's leading fashion companies. StyleVerse operates 200+ retail stores, a fast-growing digital commerce platform, and five brands across luxury, premium, fast fashion, sustainable fashion and value apparel. With 18 million customers, 28,000 employees and ₹5,600 Crores in FY24 revenue, the company has built a strong market presence. However, rising competition, changing customer expectations and fast-evolving fashion trends are pushing StyleVerse to rethink its operating model. To drive growth and improve profitability, the Board has approved a ₹200 Crore AI and Digital Transformation investment.

Despite rapid expansion, StyleVerse's retail operations still depend heavily on manual processes. Store associates spend significant time on repetitive tasks such as inventory lookups, stock checks, billing support, product searches and reporting, leaving less time for customer engagement and personalized styling. Store managers also focus heavily on operational coordination rather than coaching employees or improving store performance.

Customer expectations have also shifted. Shoppers now expect personalized recommendations, instant assistance, seamless online-offline experiences and faster responses on availability, sizing, deliveries and returns. However, customer data is fragmented across systems, making it difficult to deliver consistent, data-driven experiences. This leads to slower service, inconsistent customer journeys and missed sales opportunities.

Retail Operations also faces 42% annual employee attrition, while many employees fear AI may replace their roles. The CEO sees AI not as a cost-cutting tool, but as a catalyst to redesign work. The aim is to automate repetitive tasks, improve decision-making and help employees focus on creativity, empathy and customer engagement. StyleVerse has committed to a Zero-Layoff AI Transformation, where employees impacted by automation will be reskilled and redeployed into higher-value roles.

PwC has been asked to define the transformation strategy. Rather than recommending standalone AI tools, leadership wants to identify where AI can create the greatest business value, which retail processes to prioritize, how roles should evolve, and how AI can improve efficiency and customer experience while maintaining trust and employee confidence.

## Key questions to answer

- Where can AI create the greatest business value across StyleVerse's operations?
- Which retail processes should be prioritized for AI-driven transformation?
- How should store associate and store manager roles evolve?
- How can AI improve efficiency and customer experience while preserving customer trust and employee confidence, consistent with the Zero-Layoff commitment?
