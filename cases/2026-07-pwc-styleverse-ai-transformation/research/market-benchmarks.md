# Research notes — benchmarks for the StyleVerse AI transformation case

Sources gathered 2026-07-30. Each entry: link + one-line takeaway.

## Indian apparel market structure & discounting

- [Wazir Advisors, India Apparel Market Review 2020-25](https://wazir.in/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Indian-Apparel-Market-Review-Dynamics-and-Direction-2020-25.pdf)
  (covered by [Indian Textile Journal](https://indiantextilejournal.com/india-apparel-retail-sees-shift-in-growth-playbook/)
  and [DFU Publications](https://www.dfupublications.com/index.php/news/apparel/wazir-advisors-analysis-value-retailers-emerge-as-the-new-kings-of-indian-fashion))
  — study of 70 brands/retailers with ₹1,34,500 Cr combined FY25 revenue; discounting
  is now a *structural* drag on profitability, not a seasonal tactic.
- Same report — average listed discounts on Myntra rose from **28% (2020) to 43% (2025)**,
  with **60-73% of high-volume styles selling at over 50% markdown**. Margin leakage in
  Indian apparel is a forecasting/pricing failure, not a demand failure.
- Same report — **value retail grew at 24% CAGR FY20-FY25**, lifting its share of the
  organised market from **18% to 29%**, driven by Tier 2/3 expansion and tighter inventory
  control. Relevant: StyleVerse holds a value brand in a five-brand portfolio.

## AI impact on markdown / demand forecasting

- [Invent.ai markdown optimisation case study](https://www.invent.ai/case-study/markdown-optimization-solutions-case-study)
  — European apparel retailer cut markdown loss ~2% / lifted revenue 2.4% using elasticity +
  broken-assortment + seasonality modelling.
- [Eightgen AI retail demand forecasting case](https://eightgen.ai/case-studies/case-study-9)
  — SKU/location/day forecast accuracy 67% → 91%; **gross margin +2.8 percentage points**;
  markdown losses down $2.3M/yr.
- [ThroughPut — AI in fashion retail](https://throughput.world/blog/ai-in-fashion-retail-holiday-optimization/)
  — markdown rate fell 11 percentage points (to 24% of inventory value) within 6 months;
  repeat purchase up to 37%.
- Working benchmark adopted for this case: **2.0-2.8pp gross margin** recoverable from
  AI-led demand sensing + markdown optimisation.

## AI copilots & the novice-worker effect

- [Brynjolfsson, Li & Raymond, "Generative AI at Work", Quarterly Journal of Economics](https://academic.oup.com/qje/article/140/2/889/7990658)
  ([NBER w31161](https://www.nber.org/papers/w31161)) — AI assistance raised productivity
  **14% on average, 34% for novice/low-skilled workers, minimal for experts**.
  *This is the key finding for StyleVerse*: a high-attrition, low-tenure floor is the
  highest-return copilot population, not the lowest.
- [Stanford HAI summary](https://hai.stanford.edu/news/will-generative-ai-make-you-more-productive-work-yes-only-if-youre-not-already-great-your-job)
  — gains concentrate in workers who are not already expert; reinforces the tenure-compression framing.

## Indian retail attrition benchmarks

- [Wisemonk, Attrition Rate in India 2026](https://www.wisemonk.io/blogs/managing-attrition-in-india)
  and [TeamLease on retail staffing](https://group.teamlease.com/retail-staffing-and-recruitment-challenges-in-india/)
  — blue-collar retail attrition in India typically runs **45-75%**; broader retail sector 30-35%.
  **StyleVerse's 42% is at or below sector norm** — it is structural, not a company-specific crisis.
- Drivers cited: long hours, weekend shifts, limited career visibility, gig-economy competition.

## Quick commerce entering fashion (the emerging threat)

- [TechCrunch — Myntra M-Now](https://techcrunch.com/2024/12/05/myntra-pushes-into-india-quick-commerce-race-with-30-minute-fashion-delivery)
  — Myntra launched 30-minute fashion delivery, initially Bangalore.
- [Apparel Resources — quick commerce for fashion](https://in.apparelresources.com/business-news/retail/quick-commerce-fashion-gathers-pace-long-term-viability-comes-question/)
  and [afaqs](https://www.afaqs.com/news/mktg/from-myntra-to-ajio-how-quick-delivery-is-rewriting-fashion-retail-rules-9366081)
  — Slikk offers 60-minute delivery + try-before-you-buy; viability debated but expectations are resetting.
  *Implication*: pure-plays must build dark stores; StyleVerse's 200+ stores already sit in
  the right catchments holding real inventory — a micro-fulfilment moat if inventory is visible.

## Case arithmetic derived from the above

Assumptions flagged where StyleVerse data is not given:

| Item | Working figure | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| FY24 revenue | ₹5,600 Cr | given |
| Markdown/demand AI gain | ₹110-155 Cr/yr | 2.0-2.8pp GM on ₹5,600 Cr |
| Retail ops headcount | ~19,000 of 28,000 | assumption; store + DC operations |
| Annual exits at 42% | ~8,000 | 42% × ~19,000 |
| Direct replacement cost | ~₹40 Cr/yr | 8,000 × ~₹50,000 fully-loaded |
| Saving per 5pp attrition cut | ~₹5 Cr direct | small — the real cost is a novice-heavy floor |

Note: the direct attrition saving is deliberately *not* the headline. The material cost of
42% churn is capability (conversion on a low-tenure floor), which the copilot addresses via
the 34% novice effect — not the replacement invoice.
