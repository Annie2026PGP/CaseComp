## Prompt

**About Cipla.** Established in 1935, Cipla is a global pharmaceutical company
committed to improving access to high-quality medicines. Cipla's portfolio
spans branded generics, complex generics, OTC products and select specialty
therapies across segments such as respiratory, cardiology, central nervous
system, anti-infectives, urology and HIV. Cipla has a strong presence across
key global pharmaceutical markets, supported by trusted brands, deep
prescriber relationships, scale manufacturing capabilities, and an extensive
distribution network. As competitive intensity rises within the industry,
Cipla is increasingly focused on deepening its position in select therapies
and geographies where it has a clear and sustainable right to win, rather
than pursuing broad-based expansion.

**Case Study: AI-Enabled Prioritization with Integrated Trend Analytics.**
The global pharmaceutical market is highly competitive across most major
therapy areas. Meaningful and sustainable growth increasingly comes from
identifying the right opportunities within therapies, based not just on
current market size, but also on evolving trends shaping future demand and
competitiveness. Market data provides rich insights at both therapy and
sub-market levels, including prescription volumes, sales value, pricing
trends, competitive intensity, and brand rankings. However, transforming
this data into sharp, forward-looking, decision-oriented insights remains a
key challenge. At the same time, pharmaceutical demand and competitiveness
are increasingly influenced by broader dynamics such as: emerging disease
and treatment patterns; new drug approvals and innovation pipelines; patent
expiries and IP landscapes; environmental shifts (e.g., air quality,
climate-linked disease incidence); geopolitical and macroeconomic factors
affecting drug access and supply chains. Cipla is keen to explore how
structured analysis, supported by AI-based tools, can integrate internal
datasets with external signals to uncover actionable insights that guide
market and opportunity-space prioritization.

**Objective.** Develop a data-driven approach, supported by AI-based tools,
to help Cipla identify and prioritize opportunity spaces within the Cardiac
market where it has a strong and sustainable right to win. The analysis
should integrate current market dynamics and relevant external trends to
generate forward-looking insights on which opportunities are most likely to
outperform over the next 3-5 years and where Cipla should focus investments.

## Key questions to answer

- What are the Top 3-5 opportunities identified by the AI agent, and which
  2-3 should Cipla actively prioritize? Include key metrics/trade-offs
  observed (market size vs growth, growth vs competition, attractiveness vs
  right-to-win) and how they were resolved.
- What is Cipla's right to win in each shortlisted opportunity, and how does
  it compare with the strengths of key competitors?
- Which attractive opportunity spaces appear underpenetrated by Cipla today
  despite strong long-term potential, and what actions would be required to
  build a stronger position?
- What are the key strategic implications for Cipla? Where should the
  company double down, build capabilities, take a selective approach, or
  avoid further investment?

## Deliverables

a. Develop an AI agent that analyzes the Cardiac market dataset (shared as
   part of the case study), along with relevant external signals, to
   identify and prioritize opportunity spaces that are most likely to
   outperform the broader Cardiac market over the next 3-5 years.
   Opportunity spaces may include sub-segments, molecule classes, molecule
   combinations, treatment archetypes, or other relevant market clusters.
   Be able to explain the agent's objective, data inputs, prioritization
   framework, key metrics used, and how it evaluates trade-offs between
   market attractiveness, future potential, competitive intensity, and
   strategic fit.
b. Using the AI agent's outputs, supplemented by secondary research, answer
   the key questions above.
