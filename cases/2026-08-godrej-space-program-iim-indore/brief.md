## Prompt (verbatim)

Introducing The Space Program - a first-of-its-kind campus spacemaking
challenge where you'll reimagine a real space on the MDI campus. The winning
idea won't just remain on paper, Godrej Properties will bring it to life as
a lasting campus legacy.

**The Challenge**

Form a team of 4–5 members and identify a real space on campus that has the
potential to create a better student experience. It could be an
underutilized corner, a high-footfall area, a waiting zone, a library nook,
a hostel courtyard or any space waiting to become a memory.

Your submission should answer these three questions:

1. Which space have you chosen and why?
2. How would you redesign it to create everyday joy for students?
3. How will your idea create a lasting impact on campus and become a space
   students will continue to use and remember?

**Present It Your Way**

There are no restrictions on the format of your submission. Tell your story
in whichever way best brings your idea to life: Presentation, Video/Reels,
Sketches, storyboards, Design boards, Prototypes, models, or any other
creative medium.

**What's in it for you**

Top 2 teams from IIM Indore will earn a Pre-Placement Interview for the
Godrej Properties Gurukul Internship Program. Shortlisted teams will also
get the opportunity to present their ideas to GPL leadership at Godrej One,
Mumbai.

- **Space:** proposed by us, not user-specified — see `analysis/analysis.md`
  for the choice and rationale.

  not a slide deck — the brief explicitly allows this format.

  IIM Indore's actual buildings, exact locations and dimensions are **not

  `analysis/analysis.md`. The proposed space is deliberately chosen as a
  common, verifiable-in-person archetype (a library-to-academic-block
  connector) rather than a highly specific claim that could be wrong.
