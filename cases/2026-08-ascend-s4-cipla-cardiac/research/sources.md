Format for each source: link, one-line takeaway, and why it matters.

- [Bempedoic Acid for Lipid Management in the Indian Population: An Expert Opinion (PMC)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10040092/) — ~80% of Indians are dyslipidemic and 70% of those on statins remain LDL-uncontrolled — quantifies the unmet need behind the "add a non-statin agent" opportunity space.
- [Zydus launches Bemdac to treat LDL-Cholesterol (Indian Pharma Post)](https://www.indianpharmapost.com/drug-approval/zydus-launches-bemdac-to-treat-ldl-cholesterol-12230) — Zydus was first to launch bempedoic acid in India (May 2022, brand Bemdac) — explains why Zydus/first movers lead the "next-gen lipid lowering" space today.
- [Bempedoic acid market in India will become crowded soon with more generic launches (GlobalData)](https://www.globaldata.com/media/pharma/bempedoic-acid-market-india-will-become-crowded-soon-generic-launches-says-globaldata/) — analyst expectation that generic entrants will fragment the bempedoic acid field — supports a fast-follower entry thesis rather than "avoid."
- [Unmet Need for Further LDL-C Lowering in India despite Statin Therapy: LAI Recommendations for Bempedoic Acid (PubMed)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36082889/) — Lipid Association of India names bempedoic acid/PCSK9i/inclisiran as the next step after statin+ezetimibe fails to hit goal — sequencing logic behind prioritizing statin-ezetimibe combos first, next-gen agents second.
- [Guidelines for dyslipidemia management in India: current scenario and gaps (PMC)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9647649/) and [CSI clinical practice guidelines for dyslipidemia — executive summary (ScienceDirect)](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0019483223004698) — India-specific LDL-C targets (<55 mg/dL ASCVD, <50/<30 mg/dL extreme-risk) and explicit recommendation to add ezetimibe when statin alone doesn't reach goal — the guideline tailwind behind the Statin-Ezetimibe opportunity space.
- [Epidemiological trends and age-period-cohort effects on CVD burden attributable to ambient air pollution across BRICS (Nature Sci Reports)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-024-62295-6) — India's air-pollution-attributable CVD deaths rose ~5x from 1990-2019, faster than Brazil/Russia — external macro tailwind for hypertension/CVD demand generally.
- [Does Ambient and Indoor Air Pollution Drive Hypertension in Young/Middle-Aged Women in India? (Springer, Air Qual Atmos Health)](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11869-025-01770-z) — mean PM2.5 ~64 µg/m³ associated with 21.9% hypertension prevalence in the study cohort; PM2.5 raises hypertension odds ~1.04x — supports rising underlying AHT demand.
- [The India Hypertension Control Initiative — early outcomes in 26 districts, 2018-2020 (J Human Hypertension, Nature)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41371-022-00742-5) and [Treatment optimisation for BP with single-pill combinations in India (TOPSPIN protocol, PMC)](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11565424/) — India's national hypertension program and clinical practice are moving toward single-pill combination therapy over monotherapy as first-line — the guideline tailwind behind the ARB-CCB combination opportunity space; India has 300M+ hypertensive adults.
