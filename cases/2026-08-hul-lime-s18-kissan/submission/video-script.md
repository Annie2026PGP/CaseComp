# 60-second elevator pitch — shootable script

Format: talking head + phone-shot B-roll, cut fast. Total runtime target:
58-60 seconds. Written to be filmed on a phone in an afternoon — no studio
needed, which is on-brand for the idea itself.

Update the bracketed consumer line in Beat 2 with a **real quote from one
of your 20 interviews** before filming — that swap is what turns this from
a script into evidence the team actually did Task 1.

---

**[0:00–0:08] BEAT 1 — THE BEHAVIOUR (phone B-roll, fast cuts, no VO yet)**

Quick cuts, 1 second each: ketchup drizzled on a dosa · green chutney on a
pizza slice · someone double-dipping a momo · a spoon triple-dipped in
chutney. On-screen text, bold, appears over the cuts: **"WE ALREADY BROKE
THE RULES."**

**[0:08–0:18] BEAT 2 — THE INSIGHT (talking head, direct to camera)**

> "We talked to [N] people for this. Almost nobody we spoke to eats in
> just one cuisine anymore. [Insert one real, verbatim quote from a Task 1
> interview here — ideally someone saying something like 'I'll dip
> anything in anything, I don't really think about it.'] Ketchup already
> figured this out. Chutney hasn't."

**[0:18–0:30] BEAT 3 — THE PROBLEM (talking head + cut to a jar of
homemade/loose chutney)**

> "Right now chutney is either something your mom makes, or a jar you're
> not totally sure about. There's no chutney you'd grab the way you grab
> ketchup — trust it, squeeze it, done."

**[0:30–0:42] BEAT 4 — THE IDEA (hold up the pack mockup / show the concept
card image on a phone or printout)**

> "So here's the idea: Kissan Chutney. Same trusted squeeze bottle as
> Kissan Ketchup — Mint, Imli, Schezwan. No chopping, no gamble. Kissan
> already taught India to trust a bottle. Now it teaches India to squeeze
> its chutney the same way."

**[0:42–0:52] BEAT 5 — WHY KISSAN, WHY NOW (talking head, pick up pace)**

> "Challenger brands are already racing to own this shelf — but none of
> them have the trust Kissan already has. And nobody's made chutney feel
> as easy as ketchup — yet. This is Kissan's shelf to take, right now,
> before someone else does."

**[0:52–0:60] BEAT 6 — SIGN-OFF (talking head, on-screen tagline card)**

> "Kissan Chutney. Ab chutney bhi squeeze karo."

On-screen: pack shot / concept card, static, 2 seconds, logo + tagline.

---

## Shot list (what to actually film)

- 4-6 quick food B-roll shots (dosa+ketchup, pizza+chutney, a dip in
  progress) — can be shot at any canteen/mess/restaurant table.
- 1 shot of a store-bought or homemade chutney jar, for contrast in Beat 3.
- Talking-head footage, one consistent location, good window light.
- The concept card (`deck/assets/pack_illustration.png`, or a printout of
  Slide 2) held up or shown on-screen for Beat 4 and the sign-off card.

## What to swap before the real submission

- The bracketed quote in Beat 2 — use a real line from a real interview.
- The interview count "[N]" — your actual number (aim for the full 20).
- Consider closing on a real reaction shot from a Task 1 interviewee who
  responded well to the concept, if you got a strong one on camera during
  research — nothing sells a consumer insight like an actual consumer.
