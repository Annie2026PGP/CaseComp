## Industry context

Global fashion industry ~USD 1.7 trillion (2024). Digitally native brands have
compressed traditional 12–16 week design-to-store cycles to under four weeks.
The industry generates ~92 million tonnes of textile waste annually, 20–30% of
garments go unsold, and regulation (EU Strategy for Sustainable and Circular
Textiles, Extended Producer Responsibility) is raising expectations on
sourcing, traceability and waste. Fashion employs 75M+ people globally and
faces high attrition, widening digital skill gaps, and the need for an
AI-ready workforce. AI is viewed not as a replacement for human creativity but
as a capability that augments employees.

## Company: StyleVerse Global

Indian vertically integrated fashion company; operations across India, Europe,
North America. Serves 18M+ customers annually.

- **Revenue FY24:** ₹5,600 Cr | **Gross margin:** 42% | **EBITDA margin:** 9.5%
- **Markdown losses:** ~₹380 Cr annually
- **Workforce:** 28,000 | **Stores:** 200+ | **Manufacturing facilities:** 30
- **Digital commerce:** 35% of revenue | **Collections:** ~12/year | **SKUs:** 8,000+
- **Five brands:** SpeedStyle (fast fashion, largest/fastest-growing),
  UrbanEdge (young professionals, omnichannel), Maison Luxe (luxury,
  craftsmanship), EcoWeave (sustainable, fastest-growing), ThreadBasics (value)

**Stated pain points:**
- Trend-to-store cycle **14 weeks** vs <4 weeks for digitally native competitors
- Forecast accuracy **62%**; unsold inventory **~26%**; markdowns **>₹380 Cr**
- Customer queries take **avg 48 hours** to resolve; first-time fit accuracy
  **58%**; SpeedStyle return rate **28%**
- Average employee attrition **31%**, rising to **42%** in Retail Operations
- Only **12 hours** of structured learning annually per employee
- Information fragmented across systems; decisions depend on manual coordination
- Leadership conclusion: the **operating model**, not any single function, is
  the greatest constraint

## CEO mandate

- Objective: **15% revenue growth** next fiscal year
- **Zero-Layoff AI Transformation** — no employee loses their role to AI;
  affected employees are reskilled, upskilled and redeployed
- Board-approved **AI & Digital Transformation investment of ₹200 Crores for FY25**
- Principle agreed across the ELT: **AI should augment people, not replace them**
- Leadership view: most immediate opportunities lie in **customer-facing and
  decision-intensive functions** (Manufacturing is significant but not the
  immediate priority)
- ELT tension: CDO wants creative identity human-led; CMO believes data exists
  but capability to act on it doesn't; CHRO believes the hard part is people,
  not technology

## Deliverables

1. **Task audit** across Design & Creative, Merchandising & Planning,
   Manufacturing & Quality, Retail Operations, Marketing & Customer Experience,
   Corporate Functions. Classify activities as **Automatable, Augmentable or
   Fundamentally Human**, ensuring AI automates **up to 40% of routine work**.
   Justify what stays human-led based on creativity, judgment, empathy,
   regulatory requirements and customer impact.
2. **Prioritize functions for phase 1** justified on business impact, customer
   value, workforce size, automation potential and implementation feasibility.
   **Redesign the future operating model** — structures, roles,
   responsibilities, workflows — enabling human-AI collaboration while
   maintaining Zero-Layoff. Recommend whether AI is governed via a **centralized
   AI Center of Excellence (CoE), embedded in functions, or hybrid**, with
   rationale.
3. **Workforce strategy** — reskilling, upskilling, redeployment as routine work
   is automated. Learning journeys per employee group, new skills required, and
   how the transformation improves productivity while strengthening engagement
   and preserving institutional knowledge.
4. **Governance framework** — how AI decisions are governed; data privacy, IP
   and regulatory requirements; protecting customer trust and brand
   authenticity. Plus a **change management strategy** building employee
   confidence, aligning leadership and driving long-term adoption.
